das Zuhause

A residence in tropical brutalism with mid-century and Japanese influences, entered by a wide lit stone stair. Beneath it lies a cistern, and a glass elevator core runs through it.

Explorer yacht study

Fernweh

A 50 m sail-assist explorer yacht with two 40 m wings, a beach club, a mahogany limousine tender and a heated pool. She docks at das Zuhause’s jetty. Fernweh is German for a longing to be far away.
