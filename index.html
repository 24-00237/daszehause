Andre Parrocho

Design Studies

Interactive 3D studies of six places: a residence in tropical brutalism, the yacht that docks at its jetty, a cabin in the Alps, a studio built around a vault, a sanctuary set into a river gorge and an observatory home sunk into the Atacama. Beside them, the Models, the objects that travel between them, starting with the car. Open any one to walk through it, change it, and see it by day or by night.

Places

Design Studies

Places to live in, each one a whole world, and each one linked to the others.

Residence study

das Zuhause

A residence in tropical brutalism with mid-century and Japanese influences, entered by a wide lit stone stair, with a cistern beneath and a glass elevator core.

Enter the residence

Explorer yacht study

Fernweh

A 50 m sail-assist explorer yacht with two 40 m wings, a beach club, a mahogany limousine tender and a heated pool. She docks at das Zuhause’s jetty. Fernweh is German for a longing to be far away.

Board the yacht

Alpine cabin study

Bergruhe

A modern Alpine cabin of charred larch on a concrete base set into the slope, with a diagonal roof, a glass room cantilevered over the valley, a hearth column, a sauna and a hot tub in the snow. Bergruhe is German for mountain stillness.

Enter the cabin

Studio study

Denkraum

A quiet 43 m² studio for design and real estate work, high in a typhoon-proof Manila tower. A wall of locking drawers and a round vault door keep clients’ deeds, drawings and data, with a local AI in the vault; lifts from the garage open into a private lobby next door, where Heimweg charges below. Denkraum is German for a space to think.

Open the vault

Sanctuary study

Geborgenheit

A deep retreat set into the wall of a river gorge in the Bukidnon highlands, Mindanao: a lantern path along the water, a garden under a light well with a thin waterfall, the vault beside it, and a family wing that stays sealed until it’s needed. Geborgenheit is German for the feeling of being completely sheltered and safe.

Enter the sanctuary

Observatory home study

Sternweh

One great dome sunk into the Atacama Desert, so only its glass shows. You arrive at night on foot, along low amber lights, to a sky court, a bed under the Milky Way and a dark round star room; panelled hallways run round it in the ground. Sternweh is German for a longing for the stars.

Walk in after dark

Objects

Models

The things that travel between the places: they drive up to them, wait in their garages and keep them supplied.

Off-roader model

Heimweg

A tall electric off-roader drawn as a minimal monolith: wide flared arches over big tyres, one dark-glass canopy, and a hairline of warm light that sweeps open when you come home. It drives you between the places, waits in their garages, and parks itself under the glass at Sternweh. Heimweg is German for the way home.

Take the wheel

Water-reserve RV model

Nebelfänger

The simple RV that waits beside Heimweg in Sternweh’s showroom, kept full of water for the dry months. Nebelfänger is German for fog catcher.
