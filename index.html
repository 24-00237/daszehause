Design Studies

Interactive 3D studies of a residence in tropical brutalism, the yacht that docks at its jetty and a cabin in the Alps. Open any one to walk through it, change it, and see it by day or by night.

Residence study

das Zuhause

A residence in tropical brutalism with mid-century and Japanese influences, entered by a wide lit stone stair, with a cistern beneath and a glass elevator core.

Enter the residence

Explorer yacht study

Fernweh

A 50 m sail-assist explorer yacht with two 40 m wings, a beach club, a mahogany limousine tender and a heated pool. She docks at das Zuhause’s jetty. Fernweh is German for a longing to be far away.

Board the yacht

Alpine cabin study

Bergruhe

A modern Alpine cabin of charred larch on a concrete base set into the slope, with a diagonal roof, a glass room cantilevered over the valley, a hearth column, a sauna and a hot tub in the snow. Bergruhe is German for mountain stillness.
