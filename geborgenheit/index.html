Geborgenheit

A sanctuary study and the fifth of the Design Studies: a deep retreat set into the wall of a river gorge in the Bukidnon highlands, Mindanao. Day to day it's a quiet place to withdraw to; in an emergency it shelters the family for about a year. Geborgenheit is German for the feeling of being completely sheltered and safe.

About

Geborgenheit is a sanctuary design study by Andre Parrocho, made in November 2026 as the fifth of the Design Studies. It follows Denkraum; Heimweg, the first of the Models, is the car that brings you here and charges in its hidden bay on the plateau, and the vault comes from Denkraum. The glass lift up the light well echoes das Zuhause's glass core. Inside there are no ceiling lights at all, only uplights and lamps, so the rock and the woven wall glow from below. Sternweh, the sixth study, takes both ideas into the Atacama: a house sunk into the ground, lit only by lamps, uplights and the stars.

It’s a design exploration, not a construction drawing. Areas, food, water and power figures are rough estimates from the model’s shape. The model doesn’t check the rock, flooding, structure, ventilation or local rules.
