Bergruhe

An Alpine cabin study and the third sister to das Zuhause and Fernweh. It's a modern Alpine design: charred larch on a concrete base set into the slope, a full-glass wall to the valley and one long diagonal roof line. Bergruhe is German for mountain stillness.

About

Bergruhe is an Alpine cabin design study by Andre Parrocho, made in November 2026 as the third of the Design Studies after the das Zuhause residence and the Fernweh yacht. It carries das Zuhause's ideas up the mountain. A hearth column takes the place of the glass elevator core, the concrete base is dug into the slope, and the east skylight and flush solar roof come along too.

It’s a design exploration, not a construction drawing. Snow loads, solar yield and areas are rough estimates from the model’s shape. The model doesn’t check structure, insulation or local building rules.
