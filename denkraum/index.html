Denkraum

A studio study and the fourth of the Design Studies: one quiet room for design and real estate work, high in a typhoon-proof Manila tower. It's built around a vault that keeps clients' deeds, drawings and data. Denkraum is German for a space to think.

About

Denkraum is a studio design study by Andre Parrocho, made in November 2026 as the fourth of the Design Studies. It takes das Zuhause's ideas into the city: board-formed concrete, deep fins against the sun, storm shutters for the typhoons, and a vault wall in place of the glass core. Lifts from the garage open into a private lobby, and a door you can't see leads on into the studio; Heimweg, the first of the Models, charges in the garage below.

It’s a design exploration, not a construction drawing. Areas, loads, sound and power figures are rough estimates from the model’s shape. The model doesn’t check structure, fire escape or local building rules.
