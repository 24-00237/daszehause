# das Zuhause — Residence Study

An interactive 3D design study of das Zuhause: a residence in tropical brutalism with mid-century and Japanese influences, entered by the wide lit stone stair.

**Live:** https://24-00237.github.io/daszehause/zuhause/

Views: axonometric, perspective, eye level, plan, elevations and a half / full underground section of the cistern. Drawing styles: rendered, white model and line drawing, with Day / Dusk / Night lighting, hover labels, an Interiors switch and a design flood line.

A design study for exploring ideas — not a construction drawing.

Latest: rough build cost estimate, Study E first home, Langit sky room, library stair, roller storm shutters.

## Bergruhe — Alpine Cabin Study

Third study: a modern Alpine cabin of charred larch on a concrete base set into the slope, with a diagonal roof (or a horizontal or gable roof to compare), a glass room cantilevered over the valley, a hearth column, a wood-fired sauna and an outdoor hot tub. Seasons from summer to deep winter, alpenglow and snowfall, and walk-throughs.

**Live:** https://24-00237.github.io/daszehause/bergruhe/
