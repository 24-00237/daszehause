# das Zuhause — Residence Study

An interactive 3D design study of das Zuhause: a residence in tropical brutalism with mid-century and Japanese influences, entered by the wide lit stone stair.

**Live:** https://24-00237.github.io/daszehause/zuhause/

Views: axonometric, perspective, eye level, plan, elevations and a half / full underground section of the cistern. Drawing styles: rendered, white model and line drawing, with Day / Dusk / Night lighting, hover labels, an Interiors switch and a design flood line.

A design study for exploring ideas — not a construction drawing.

## Heimweg — Off-Roader Study

Fourth study: an original electric luxury off-roader drawn as a minimal monolith. It stands 2.75 m tall and 2.52 m wide over flared arches, on 1.2 m tyres whose width you can choose. It has a glass canopy that narrows slightly toward the roof, a long bonnet, and a warm full-width light signature with a "welcome home" sweep. Working rear lights include tail, brake, sequential indicators, hazards and reverse.

Expedition kit: snorkel, winch, mud flaps, rock lights, a heated windscreen strip, a spare wheel and high-lift jack on the side-hinged tailgate, and an optional fuel can. Camp mode opens a side awning, a pop-up roof and a pull-out kitchen, and the car kneels when its doors open. Cameras include a see-through-bonnet ground view. Scenes: a studio, Bergruhe's drive and das Zuhause at night. It's also parked on Bergruhe's driveway.

**Live:** https://24-00237.github.io/daszehause/heimweg/

## Denkraum — Studio Study

Fifth study: a quiet 43 m² studio for design and real estate work on the 38th floor of a typhoon-proof tower in Manila (or a mid-rise condo). Its signature is the vault: a back wall of locking narra drawers for deeds and drawings, a round steel vault door with a brass wheel (or a square one, or one hidden in the drawers), and a fire-rated vault room behind it with the server rack, a local AI and a NAS, so client data never leaves the room.

Five zones in one room: a model bench on the north glass, a meeting table with a presentation screen, a lounge, a reading nook in the glass corner and a nap corner with a linen curtain and a warm lamp. Box-in-box soundproofing and a hidden door from a private lift lobby. The tower stands on a paved plaza over one level of parking, where Heimweg charges by the lifts among the other cars. In the cutaway, the floors around and above the studio turn to X-ray instead of disappearing. Deep concrete fins, a sun ledge, planters and steel storm shutters for the heat, the monsoon and the typhoons. Scenes from early morning to city night, monsoon rain and a typhoon, with focus and nap light.

**Live:** https://24-00237.github.io/daszehause/denkraum/
