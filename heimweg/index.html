Heimweg

An electric luxury off-roader drawn as a minimal monolith: one calm block, tough below and glass above, with a hairline of warm light front and back. Heimweg is German for the way home. It’s the car that drives you between das Zuhause and Bergruhe.

About

Heimweg is a car design study by Andre Parrocho, made in November 2026 as the fourth of the Design Studies after das Zuhause, Fernweh and Bergruhe. It’s an original design: a squared-off monolith on short overhangs, a single dark-glass canopy, flush glass and hidden handles, tough matte surfaces low down, and a warm light line that sweeps open when you come home. It also lives in Bergruhe’s garage.

A design exploration, not an engineering drawing. Dimensions, range and angles are rough estimates from the model.
