Sternweh

An observatory home and the sixth of the Design Studies: a house under the stars and a place of pilgrimage. One great dome is sunk into the Atacama Desert in Chile so that only its glass shows above the ground. You arrive only at night, on foot, following low lights across the desert. Sternweh is German for a longing for the stars.

About

Sternweh is an observatory home by Andre Parrocho, made in November 2026 as the sixth of the Design Studies. Heimweg, the first of the Models, brings you here: it stops out in the desert, you walk the last stretch, and it parks itself under the glass in its own showroom. The sunken design and the rule of uplights and lamps come from Geborgenheit, and the cistern is a smaller cousin of das Zuhause’s. The RV that waits beside Heimweg will become Nebelfänger, a Model of its own.

It’s a design exploration, not a construction drawing. Areas, water, power and heat figures are rough estimates from the model’s shape. The model doesn’t check the structure, the ground, earthquakes or local rules. The sky is drawn roughly as it really is from 24.6° south.
